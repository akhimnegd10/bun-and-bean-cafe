# BUN & BEAN Café

## About the Brand
BUN & BEAN is a modern café serving specialty coffee, refreshing drinks, and fresh baked goods.

## Target Audience
Young people, students, and coffee lovers.

## Brand Personality
- Modern
- Warm
- Simple
- Friendly

## Color Palette

- Cream: #F6EBDD
- Caramel: #C77D52
- Sage Green: #79836B

## Brand Slogan
Coffee, Fresh Bakes, Good Moments.

## Project Goal
Create a modern, responsive café website and a consistent visual identity.
